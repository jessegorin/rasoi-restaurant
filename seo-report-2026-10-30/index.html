Our game plan

Most of your searches come from Edison, but most of your website speaks to Jersey City. These steps close that gap.

1

Point your dish pages at Edison too

RECOMMENDED

All 29 dish and menu pages are written for Jersey City, for example “Best Butter Chicken in Jersey City, NJ.” Edison is your most searched town, so Edison versions of your top pages would let Google show them there.

Jersey City: 29 pages+ Butter chicken, Edison+ Biryani, Edison+ Catering, Edison+ Halal, Edison
2

Give Jersey City its own town page

RECOMMENDED

There are town pages for Edison, Iselin, Metuchen, Hoboken and four more, but none for Jersey City, home of Rasoi I. A page with its own address, hours and menu helps it win searches like “Indian restaurant Jersey City.”

3

Make the homepage say one thing

YOUR CALL

The homepage names three different places for the same listing. Once you pick the wording, the title, description and headline can all be matched to it, for example:

Today: Dayton / Monmouth Junction / Jersey City AreaIndian Restaurant in Jersey City, Edison & Dayton NJ

Google’s own listings use Jersey City, Woodbridge Township and Monmouth Junction, so whichever names you choose, matching them across your website and listings helps.

4

Fill out your Google categories

RECOMMENDED

Categories tell Google which searches to show you for. None of your three listings is marked halal, which is why none of them shows up for “halal food.” Rasoi III is your catering hall, but Google lists it as a noodle, seafood and soup shop instead.

Indian restaurantBiryani restaurant+ Halal restaurant+ North Indian restaurant+ Caterer+ Banquet hall (Rasoi III)Review at Rasoi III: Noodle shop, Seafood restaurant, Soup shop
✓

Your ads now skip other restaurants’ searches

DONE

On October 30 we blocked searches for Deccan Spice, Hot Breads, Moghul and Amritsari, plus “recipe,” “how to make,” “jobs” and “hiring,” and updated both ads with “Order Butter Chicken Online,” “Order Pickup Online” and “Order Direct.”
