The Takeaways

Highlights & Lowlights

September 28 to October 27, both locations combined unless noted

Highlights

  • $288 in ad spend brought in $670 in online orders, a 2.3x return. Every $1 spent came back as $2.33 in sales.
  • Jersey City is the standout: 9 online orders and $439.70 in sales on $142.60 spend, a 3.1x return.
  • Clicks are inexpensive at about $0.35 each across 823 clicks.
  • The ads drive more than online orders: 89 phone calls, 226 requests for directions and an estimated 206 store visits. None of these are counted in the revenue above.
  • The average online order from an ad was $47.87.

Lowlights

  • Edison is returning 1.6x: 5 orders and $230.50 on $145.44 spend, about half of Jersey City's return.
  • Most clicks come from people already searching for "Rasoi" by name. The ads are capturing loyal guests well but reaching few new diners. Generic searches like "indian restaurants near me" got clicks with no orders.
  • Google rates both ads "Poor" for ad strength, which limits how often and how well they show.
  • The Edison ad describes the restaurant as being in the Iselin area, which does not match the Edison location guests are searching for.
  • Both campaigns use their full $5/day budget, so on busy days the ads stop showing before the day is over.

Recommendations

Keyword Suggestions

Changes to who sees the ads

  • Add Rasoi name keywords for each location, such as "rasoi edison", "rasoi jersey city", "rasoi menu" and "rasoi order online". These are the searches already producing orders, so targeting them directly keeps that traffic steady and inexpensive.
  • Add dish plus city keywords to reach new diners: "butter chicken edison", "biryani jersey city", "indian takeout edison nj" and "indian food delivery jersey city". Butter chicken is the top seller, so pairing it with each town is a natural next step.
  • Pause or trim keywords spending without orders: "indian food catering" ($21.45, no orders), "tikka masala" in Jersey City ($15.85, no orders) and "indian buffet" in Jersey City ($10.76, no orders). Freeing up that budget lets the proven keywords show more often.
  • Add negative keywords so the ads skip searches that will not lead to an order: other restaurants' names (for example "amritsari"), plus "recipe", "how to make", "jobs" and "hiring". The account has no negative keywords today.
  • Give catering its own approach: catering searches get clicks but rarely become online orders, since catering usually starts with a phone call. Pointing catering keywords to a catering page or call button would capture those leads.
Recommendations

Ad Changes to Drive More Orders

Changes to what the ads say and how they run

  • Fix the Edison location wording: change Best Indian Food In The Iselin Area to Best Indian Food In Edison. Order Online.
  • Swap vague headlines for specific ones. Replace Suitable for unlimited food and Suitable for comfort food with headlines like Order Butter Chicken Online, Fresh Naan & Biryani and Order Pickup Online.
  • Name the town in a headline for each location: Indian Food in Edison, NJ and Indian Food in Jersey City. Location-specific ads tend to earn more clicks from nearby diners.
  • Add "Order Direct" to the Edison ad. The Jersey City ad already has it, and Jersey City returns nearly twice as much per dollar.
  • Raise ad strength from "Poor" to "Good" by filling all 15 headline slots with distinct messages (dishes, pickup, delivery, catering, halal, location).
  • Add the food photos uploaded on October 25 to both campaigns as image extensions so the ads stand out on mobile, where 93% of clicks happen.
  • Consider more budget for Jersey City. It returns $3.08 for every $1 and runs out of budget daily. Moving from $5/day to $8 to $10/day would let it show on more searches.
